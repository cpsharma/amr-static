The Ledger · 2026-07-14

On testliner, and patience

European testliner has spent the month doing what it does best: very little, slowly. Mill order books are adequate rather than full, and sellers who need the tonne moved are quietly finding the bid a few euros below where they would like it. Buyers with patience are being rewarded; buyers with deadlines are paying for them.

UK recovered grades have firmed at the margin. Export demand for OCC is steady, collections seasonally thin, and the arithmetic favours the holder for now. We do not expect drama — merely a market that has remembered which way gravity points in summer.

Freight, for once, behaves. Short-sea rates from UK ports are stable, deep-sea space to the subcontinent is available at sensible money, and transit times are being honoured more often than not. Long may it bore us.

The desk's position is unchanged: prime and side-run testliner placed promptly, redundant board stock bought outright, collections arranged within the week.
