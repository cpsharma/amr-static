Paper & Board · The Primary Desk

Every grade. Every scale.

Prime and secondary paper and board, traded across the UK, Europe and North America. We sell to mills, printers and converters; we buy redundant, surplus and recovered material outright.

I. — Grades Handled

Prime reels & sheets
Testliner, kraftliner, fluting; coated and uncoated woodfree; folding boxboard and other boards. Full production runs and regular programmes.
Stocklots & side-runs
Over-makings, trial runs, non-standard widths and grammages, single-lot clearances. Described honestly, sold without pretence.
Redundant & surplus
Aged stock, cancelled orders, customer rejections, warehouse clearances — purchased outright, in any volume.
Recovered grades
Printed and unprinted waste, converter offcuts, baled grades for mill consumption. Regular collection programmes arranged.

II. — Selling & Buying

We sell

Mills and converters buy from us because the material arrives as described. Specifications are stated plainly, loading is photographed where useful, and shipping documents are handled by people who read them.

Lots currently available

We buy

Holding redundant stock costs money every week. We make fair open-market offers, settle promptly, and collect anywhere in the UK and Europe with our own arranged haulage. One pallet or a full warehouse; the courtesy is the same.

Offer material to the desk

III. — Collection of Redundant Stock

Send a line describing the material — grade, approximate tonnage, location, a photograph if convenient. The desk replies with an indication quickly, usually the same day. If the numbers work, we arrange collection at our cost and settle on agreed weights.
