Terms of Trade

How the house trades.

The terms below are indicative and describe our customary practice. Every contract is concluded individually, in writing, and the concluded contract alone governs.

I. Contract Formation

Business is concluded by written sales or purchase confirmation issued by AMR Universal Ltd and accepted by the counterparty. Quotations are invitations to treat and remain subject to prior sale. No liability attaches to indications given by telephone or messaging until confirmed in writing.

II. Incoterms

We trade under Incoterms® 2020. Customary bases: EXW, FOB, CIF and DAP. The applicable term, named place and any variations are stated on each confirmation.

III. Payment

Customary instruments: irrevocable letter of credit at sight opened through a first-class bank; cash against documents (CAD) through agreed banks; and telegraphic transfer against presentation of documents. New relationships trade on secured instruments until a course of dealing is established. Title passes on cleared payment.

IV. Weights & Franchise

Unless otherwise agreed, invoicing follows certified loading weights (mill scale or weighbridge, tickets supplied). A franchise of one half of one per cent applies to weight differences; claims lie only for shortfall beyond the franchise, evidenced by certified destination weights.

V. Inspection & Quality Claims

Buyers are invited to inspect before shipment; independent inspection (e.g. SGS or equivalent) may be arranged at buyer's cost. Quality claims must be notified in writing within fourteen days of arrival, with photographs and, where appropriate, surveyor's findings, before the material is processed. Material processed is material accepted. Stocklot and secondary material is sold as described, without mill guarantee, and this is reflected in its price.

VI. Delivery & Force Majeure

Shipment periods are given in good faith and observed with care. Neither party is liable for delay or non-performance arising from events beyond reasonable control, including mill stoppages, port congestion, vessel delays and acts of authority; affected obligations are suspended for the duration of the event.

VII. Jurisdiction

All contracts are governed by the laws of England & Wales. Disputes not resolved amicably fall to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England & Wales, save where a confirmation provides for arbitration.

Indicative terms — every contract concluded individually. Nothing on this page constitutes an offer capable of acceptance.
