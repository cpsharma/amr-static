Metals · The Second Desk

Ferrous and non-ferrous, traded with the same discipline.

Scrap and secondary metals move through the same house, on the same word. Smaller desk; same standards.

I. — Materials Handled

Steel
HMS 1&2, shredded, plate and structural. Prepared to ISRI basis, loaded in containers or bulk, weighbridge tickets supplied.
Aluminium
Extrusions, castings, used beverage cans, wheels. Clean, segregated, assay on request.
Cast iron
Machine breaks, engine blocks, demolition arisings. Drained, free of contamination.

II. — How the Desk Works

Yards and works sell to us on regular programmes or single liftings. Export buyers take containerised and bulk material against the usual instruments. Descriptions are honest, weights are settled on the bridge, and claims — rare — are dealt with on evidence.

The metals desk observes the Scrap Metal Dealers Act 2013 and the waste-carrier regime; licence particulars appear under Compliance & Accreditations as they are granted.
